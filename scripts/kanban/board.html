MatchPoint AI — Final Project

MatchPoint AI is a smartphone-controlled tennis motion trainer built for the 2026 From Idea To Reality — App Using AI course. Expo and native phone sensors stream through a Node.js and Socket.io broker to a TypeScript and Three.js desktop trainer with calibrated racket motion, stroke analysis, and custom ball physics.
