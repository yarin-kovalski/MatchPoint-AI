01 Set your ready position +

Face the screen and hold your phone securely. Align the on-screen racket with the ghost racket and tap Calibrate. Recalibrate if your grip or standing position changes.

02 Time your forehand & backhand +

Watch the incoming ball, prepare on the matching side, and swing through its approach after the bounce. Start gently. Use the early, on-time, or late feedback to adjust the next swing.

03 Shape spin, slice & height +

A low-to-high path develops topspin. For slice, use a slightly open face and a controlled cutting motion. Face angle affects launch height; pace and follow-through help shape depth. Phone-based technique metrics are estimates.

04 Work on placement +

Use Court Vision to see the first bounce. Try deep cones for length, short cones for touch, and regular cones for variety. With wind on, watch its direction and allow for the ball's drift.

05 Pause, review, repeat +

Stop pauses feeding; Resume restarts it. Single shot feeds one ball at a time. Finish session stops automatic feeding and lets the current ball resolve before opening your feedback.
