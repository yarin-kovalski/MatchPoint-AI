03 / PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE

Pick a target.
Read the air.

Knock down cone groups in deep, regular or short-shot practice. They stand back up in position so you can build consistency.

Bring wind into play. Choose direction and light, medium or strong conditions to change the ball’s drift and pace.

05 / YOUR NEXT MOVE

The court
is yours.

A computer. A phone. Room to move.
Here’s your path to the first ball.

  1. 01

    Get Expo Go

    Install on your phone. Join the same Wi-Fi as your computer.

    Download Expo Go ↗
  2. 02

    Scan & connect

    Scan the QR with the iPhone camera or Expo Go scanner on Android. In MatchPoint, tap Connect, then Start.

  3. 03

    Hold. Align. Calibrate.

    Face your computer. Hold your phone firmly with clear space around you. Match the racket to the ghost outline and tap Calibrate on your phone.

  4. 04

    Play your first ball

    Choose Forehand or Backhand on the PC. Prepare early, meet the ball after its bounce, and follow through smoothly. Use timing feedback for your next shot.
